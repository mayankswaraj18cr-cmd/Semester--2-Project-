▸ CAD Commands Used

LINECreates straight lines
CIRCLECreates circular holes
OFFSETCreates parallel lines
TRIMRemoves unwanted portions
DIMAdds dimensions

▸ Procedure

  1. Open CAD software
  2. Set units to millimeters (mm)
  3. Draw rectangle: 120 mm × 80 mm
  4. Mark hole centers
  5. Draw circles — diameter 20 mm
  6. Apply dimensions
  7. Add center lines
  8. Save drawing as Mounting_Plate_2D.dwg
